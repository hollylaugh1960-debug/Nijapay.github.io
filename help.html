Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about JODEK PAY.

How do I create a JODEK PAY account?

Click the Register option and provide your name, phone number, email and password. After successful registration, you can log in to your account.

How do I fund my wallet?

Log in to your dashboard and select the Fund Wallet option. Follow the available payment instructions to add money to your JODEK PAY wallet.

How do I buy airtime?

Select Airtime from the services section, choose your network, enter the phone number and amount, then continue with the payment.

How do I purchase data?

Select Data from the services section. Choose your network, phone number, plan type and data package, then continue with the payment.

How do I pay for Cable TV?

Select Cable TV, choose your provider, enter your Smartcard or IUC number and verify the customer. Select your subscription package and continue.

How do I pay my electricity bill?

Select Electricity, choose your electricity provider and meter type. Enter your meter number and payment amount, then continue.

How do I purchase an examination PIN?

Select Examination PINs, choose the examination you need and enter the quantity. Your wallet will be used for payment.

What should I do if my transaction fails?

First, check your wallet balance and transaction history. If your account was debited but the service was not received, contact JODEK PAY support with your transaction details.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds depend on the type and status of the transaction. Contact support with your transaction reference so the transaction can be investigated.

I cannot log in to my account. What should I do?

Check that your email and password are correct. If you have forgotten your password, use the password recovery option. You can also contact support if the problem continues.

Still Need Help?

Our support team is available to assist you with account and transaction issues.
