Privacy Policy

Your privacy and the security of your information are important to us.

1. Introduction

Jodek Pay is committed to protecting the privacy and security of information provided by our users. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect information when you use Jodek Pay.

2. Information We Collect

When you create or use a Jodek Pay account, we may collect information such as:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Account information
  • Transaction information
  • Service usage information

3. How We Use Your Information

Information collected may be used to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Process transactions and services.
  • Maintain your wallet and transaction records.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Improve our services and platform.
  • Protect our users and prevent fraudulent activities.

4. Payment Information

Payments and wallet funding may be processed through authorized payment providers. Jodek Pay does not intend to store sensitive card or banking credentials unnecessarily.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

6. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may only be shared where necessary to provide requested services, process transactions, comply with legal requirements, or protect the security of Jodek Pay and its users.

7. Cookies and Technologies

Jodek Pay may use browser storage, cookies or similar technologies where necessary to maintain sessions, improve functionality and provide a better user experience.

8. Your Information

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information associated with your account, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

9. Children's Privacy

Jodek Pay is not intended to be used by individuals who are not legally permitted to create an account or use financial services.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any significant changes will be reflected on this page.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact Jodek Pay through our Contact page .
